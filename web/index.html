You stay in control

Look first.
Decide second.

  1. 01 / SCAN

    See what’s there.

    Scan your chosen scope. Inspect candidates, explanations, and uncertainty. Scanning writes local audit records and does no cleaning.

    diskroom scan ./projects
  2. 02 / REVIEW

    Make the call.

    Adjust the goal and selection. Read what changes, what could go wrong, and how to restore an environment. Confirm a specific plan.

    diskroom clean --scan <run-id>
  3. 03 / CLEAN

    Know when to stop.

    Recheck before each operation. Measure actual free space afterwards. Stop starting new work as soon as the goal is met.

    diskroom history <run-id>
A good tool knows its limits.

Your machine.
Your decisions.

Local by design.

No account, cloud service, AI model, or telemetry is needed for a local scan. Your file paths stay on your machine.

An audit, not an undo button.

Compressed records in .diskroom explain the scan, plan, and results. Deletion may be irreversible. Restoration can require network access and credentials.

Conservative when unsure.

Protected and unknown resources do not become expendable because a goal is ambitious. DiskRoom can finish with a goal unmet.
